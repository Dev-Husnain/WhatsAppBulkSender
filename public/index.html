Start the sender on your PC

This page sends through a small program on your own computer, logged in to your own WhatsApp. Nobody else's number is ever used.

Download for Windows
  1. Double-click WhatsAppBulkSender.exe. Nothing to install.
  2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.
  3. This page connects by itself. Then scan the QR code with your WhatsApp.

Needs Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (already on most PCs). On Mac or Linux, run it from source.

Advanced: sender address

If the browser asks to allow access to devices on your local network, click Allow.

Link your WhatsApp

  1. Open WhatsApp on your phone
  2. Go to Settings → Linked devices
  3. Tap Link a device and scan this code

You only need to do this once.
